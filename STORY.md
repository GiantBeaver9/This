## 1. Premise — **[LOCKED]**

- **The title *this.l*** is the drawing itself: two friends passing a doodle back and forth. The world is a
  hand-drawn, pixel-art plane — a 2D creation living on a 3D planet.
- **Phil** — a top-hatted figure who looks like one of the drawn zombies — **steals the magic pencil** that
  draws things to life, escaping from **Lincoln High** at the opening. With it he can **draw enemies into
  existence.** He is the artist-villain; every enemy in the game is, diegetically, *something Phil drew.*
- **The player** (the Human, and the 3 alt characters `CHARACTERS.md`) chases Phil across a real **NorCal
  road trip — Lincoln → Rocklin → Roseville → Sacramento → the airport → the causeway → Dixon → Vallejo →
  Marin → the Golden Gate → San Francisco** — to take the pencil back before he finishes what he's drawing.

## 2. The stakes escalation — **[LOCKED]**

- Early on Phil is just **doodling thugs** to slow you down (Areas 1–2: Regulars, the first big-version boss
  as "proof of the pencil," `AREAS.md` §1.6).
- Mid-journey the drawings get **more elaborate and dangerous** (Areas 3–4: snipers, monkeys, ninjas, heavy
  weapons) — Phil is getting **better at drawing**, and the pencil is running dull.
- **The reveal (rooftop, `VIGNETTES.md` finale):** Phil has reached the **Salesforce Tower** and found the
  **Holy Sharpener** — the one thing that can keep the dull pencil drawing forever. His monologue: he'll
  **"bring 2D chaos to this 3D planet."** The tower **sways** as the world starts to slip between dimensions.

## 3. The climax — **[LOCKED]**

- On the swaying rooftop, Phil **draws his greatest hits** — reprises of the bosses and enemies you've beaten
  (`BOSSES.md` §5.1 reprise summons) — while the **dull pencil forces him to keep sharpening**, the only
  moment he's vulnerable.
- **The end:** the finisher is **replaced by a scripted pencil-laser** — the player turns Phil's own pencil on
  him, firing a laser that ends him (`BOSSES.md` §5.1, `TUNING.md` §7). The drawing is reclaimed; the 3D world
  is safe.
- **[LOCKED] Outro:** a **~15 s hand-drawn still-clip epilogue** (same style as the intro, `AUDIO.md` §5) — the
  two friends' drawing settles back into a doodle, one last creator-voiced line — then **scrolling credits**
  over the finale music (`AUDIO.md` §2). After credits: return to the **title screen**, with **Endless Mode**
  now the standing replay draw (`UI.md` §5). No post-credits scene.

## 4. Tone — **[LOCKED]**

- **Comedic, warm, homemade** — matching the creator-voiced intro and the transient-gore slapstick
  (`VFX.md` §1). Not grimdark; the violence is cartoon-doodle violence (enemies pop into red pixels and clear).
- The **"two friends passing a drawing"** intimacy is the emotional core — the road trip is a love letter to
  the real NorCal route, rendered as a living sketch.

## 5. Where story is delivered (no extra cutscene budget) — **[LOCKED]**

| Beat | Delivery vehicle | Doc |
|---|---|---|
| Premise / pencil theft | **Opening cinematic** (~1:40, 5 voiced still-clips, creator voice) | `STAGES.md` §1b, `AUDIO.md` §5 |
| Each new threat | **3–5 s stage vignette** (enemies act out the mechanic) | `VIGNETTES.md` |
| Escalation | **environmental** — the route visibly changes; enemies get fancier | `AREAS.md` |
| The reveal & stakes | **Phil's rooftop monologue** (menacing laughter, sway foreshadow) | `VIGNETTES.md`, `BOSSES.md` §5.1 |
| Resolution | **the pencil-laser finisher** + short outro | `BOSSES.md` §5.1 |

- **No mid-stage dialogue system, no branching narrative** (campaign is linear, `STAGES.md` §5). Story assets
  = the intro clips + Phil's VO + the vignette scripts (all reuse existing art). This keeps the narrative
  **lightweight** and fully inside the existing asset budget.
