# this.l — Vignette Plan (all stages)

> **The teaching device** (`STAGES.md` §1c): a **brief ~3–5s vignette** at the start of a stage where enemies
> *act out* the new mechanic/enemy, so the player sees what's coming and how to beat it. Reuses enemy/weapon
> art (cheap). This doc tracks **every stage's vignette** so none are missed.
>

## Notes
- **Opener exception refined:** the first stage *does* get a light vignette (the Zebra punch) — it just teaches
  the punch rather than a weapon/enemy. This supersedes the earlier "first stage has no vignette."
- **Rule:** a stage that introduces **no new mechanic** (pure ramp) can **skip** the vignette. Fill the
  `[ITERATE]` rows as those stages' rosters finalize; each should still open by *showing* its new thing.
- Vignette production is **cheap** — scripted setups reusing existing enemy/weapon/animation art.
